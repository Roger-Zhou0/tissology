3. Train and evaluate a version

Train a separate classifier from approved corrections and existing development examples. Review its validation results for improvements and regressions.

Training and evaluation details

Fit a small C=10 logistic classifier on the 4,500 original training examples, replacing approved labels and giving those examples weight 5. Keep all other examples at weight 1. The encoder stays frozen.

Each candidate is compared with a new train-only control on the reused 900-image development validation set. The deployed classifier was fitted on all 5,400 development labels and is not that control. These results support development decisions, not fresh generalization.

4. Eligibility for a new study

A configured fresh test compares the frozen candidate with the deployed model on unused images. Only candidates that pass the frozen checks can open a separate new study. Existing studies, predictions and audit intervals stay bound to their original models.
